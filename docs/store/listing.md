## Full description (2339/4000)

```
Before You Post checks a photo for things you might not want to share, and lets you hide them - all on your device.

Screenshots and photos give away more than people notice. A conversation screenshot can contain a phone number, an email address and a home address. A screenshot of a terminal can contain an API key. A holiday photo can contain a stranger's face. A picture of a parcel can contain a QR code that resolves to your details.

Before You Post finds those things, shows you what it found, and lets you decide what to hide.


How it works

1. Choose a photo, or take one.
2. The app scans it for faces, text, and QR or barcodes.
3. Text is checked against rules for phone numbers, email addresses, web links, card numbers and security codes, addresses, vehicle number plates and API keys.
4. You review everything found, and choose what to hide and how.
5. Tap Protect, and the app produces a new copy with those areas obscured.

Your original photo is never modified.


What it finds

• Faces
• Phone numbers
• Email addresses
• Web links
• Card numbers, validated with a checksum to cut down on false alarms
• Card security codes, where the card labels them
• Addresses, including house and flat numbers and postcodes
• Vehicle number plates
• API keys, access tokens and other credentials
• QR codes and barcodes, with the decoded contents shown so you can judge them yourself


Three ways to hide something

• Blackout, a solid block, and the strongest option
• Pixelate, visible blocks
• Blur, a soft smear

You can choose per item. Text defaults to blackout, because blurred or pixelated text is a weaker guarantee than a solid block.


Draw your own

Automatic detection is never complete. A drawing tool lets you cover anything the scan missed, and those areas are treated exactly like detected ones.


On your device

• Photos are analysed on your device
• No account and no sign-in
• No ads
• No backend, so there is no server to send anything to

The app uses Google ML Kit for detection, which sends diagnostic and usage information about those libraries to Google. Your images are not part of that. The privacy policy explains this in full.


What it cannot promise

Detection is automatic and imperfect. It will sometimes miss things, and sometimes flag things that are not sensitive. The app describes what it finds as potential risks, always lets you review before anything is hidden, and gives you a manual tool for the rest.

Always check a protected image yourself before you share it.


Before You Post is open source: https://github.com/ALLEXCEED360/Before-You-Post
```
